ERA is a community-edited forum for discussion and interpretation of peer-reviewed publications pertaining to epigenetic reprogramming and its effects on aging. These interpretations may include associations between reprogramming interventions (or their absence), epigenetic marks, and rejuvenation, lifespan, healthspan or other aging-related outcomes. These interpretations are purely for research purposes. A finding of no interpretation does not necessarily indicate lack of relevance for any specific intervention or mark. Interpretations are not presented in ranked order of potential or predicted importance. These interpretations make no promise or guarantee of any clinical or biological benefit (or lack thereof).
